Privacy Policy

When you submit a form on this site (contact, intake, inquiry, or email signup), the information you enter — such as your name, email address, company, and message — is sent to us via Web3Forms, a form delivery service, so we can respond to you and deliver what you requested. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Payments are processed entirely by Stripe or PayPal on their own secure checkout pages. We never see, collect, or store your card or bank details. Stripe and PayPal handle your payment data under their own privacy policies.

Storage in your browser

If you use the project builder to assemble a project, your selections are kept in your browser's session storage so they survive scrolling and a page refresh, and are sent to us only if you submit the inquiry form. They are cleared when you close the tab. The site also stores small amounts of data in local storage strictly for functionality, such as preventing duplicate form submissions. This data stays on your device.

Analytics

This site does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics trackers.

Your information

To request access to or deletion of any personal information you have submitted, email partnerships@worldwidewmal.com and we will respond promptly.
